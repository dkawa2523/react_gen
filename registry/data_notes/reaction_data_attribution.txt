Reaction-data attribution and redistribution review
Checked: 2026-11-01

Scope
-----
This notice describes the sources represented in the working Registry. It is
not a blanket licence for the Registry or a scientific qualification. Source
citations, original units, native row identifiers, transformations and reviewed
applicability remain in the individual records. Distribution decisions must
also consider the actual material being distributed: a numerical fact, an
independently encoded formula, a source's selected compilation, an original
table, its explanatory text, and a source PDF are different materials.

The Registry does not include the original journal PDFs, page images, or table
images for the literature references below. A citation or a publicly accessible
author manuscript is not, by itself, a redistribution licence. No permission
was requested from authors or publishers as part of this review.

https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19940013151
NASA NTRS marks the original as a US Government work with public use permitted.

Journal numerical records

The following are scientific references and extraction scopes. No open

tables was established. Article rights and data-only reuse are considered
separately in docs/source_license_policy.md.

Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology A 32, 021303 (2014).
https://doi.org/10.1116/1.4853675

source rows, R38-R50 and R119-R137. Generic source rules were expanded over

redistribution decision; an author's public proof is not a permission grant.

Elsevier's permissions FAQ distinguishes use of numerical data to make a new
representation from reproduction of an original figure or table, and requests
source acknowledgement even where permission is unnecessary:
https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/copyright/permissions
This is relevant to the isolated Elsevier numerical declarations above. It is
not an open licence for the articles or a universal rule for other publishers.
Crossref's TDM-only licence metadata is not a redistribution grant.

NASA/JPL kinetics evaluations
-----------------------------
J. B. Burkholder, R. L. Caravan, R. E. Huie, V. L. Orkin, C. J. Percival,
R. Sander, S. P. Sander and D. M. Wilmouth, Chemical Kinetics and Photochemical
Data for Use in Atmospheric Studies, Evaluation No. 20, JPL Publication 25-1,
Jet Propulsion Laboratory, Pasadena (2025).
https://science.jpl.nasa.gov/projects/data-evaluation/
https://doi.org/10.48577/jpl.KFQ0TZ
Copyright 2025, California Institute of Technology.
U.S. Government sponsorship acknowledged.

Working Registry scope: four current evaluated/derived numerical expressions
associated with Notes A3, A7 and A12; two historical expressions associated with
JPL Publication 19-5 Note A3 are retained separately. The original PDF, page
layout and explanatory text are not distributed. The evaluated loss rate,
branching fractions, derived nominal branch rate and fine-state limitations
remain distinguishable. The notice in the original PDF and Caltech/JPL's
copyright policy do not establish a blanket public-domain classification:
https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/caltechjpl-privacy-policies-and-important-notices/
Permission for redistribution of a larger selected collection was not
established. Numerical factual extracts are not classified as prohibited
solely because the containing document has a copyright notice.

Pending cross-section references
-------------------------------
References to LXCat, NIST SRD 107, Itikawa, Christophorou/Olthoff, Song and other
sources with no acquired numerical curve are acquisition targets or citations.
They are not distribution of the referenced datasets. In particular the
CC BY-NC oxygen workbook, LXCat numerical tables and original source PDFs are
not bundled in registry/assets. Their own terms must be checked before adding
them or distributing them in an analysis result.

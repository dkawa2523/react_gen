NIST Standard Reference Data: source attribution and scope
Reviewed 2026-11-01

The Registry cites selected numerical facts from NIST Chemistry WebBook
(SRD 69), Atomic Spectra Database (SRD 78), Computational Chemistry Comparison
and Benchmark DataBase (SRD 101), and Basic Atomic Spectroscopic Data (SRD 108).
The per-record references, versions where known, units and transformations
remain in the corresponding species/reaction records.

NIST states for its Standard Reference Data compilations:

Copyright protection on this compilation of data has been secured by the
Secretary of the U.S. Department of Commerce on behalf of the United States
in the United States and all countries that are parties to the Universal
Copyright Convention, pursuant to Section 290(e) of Title 15 of the
United States Code.

Source: https://www.nist.gov/open/license

This project independently selects factual numerical entries for its own
per-species representation. It does not include NIST page archives, database
layouts, bulk source tables or spectral images. NIST's compilation notices and
source-specific rights remain with their respective owners. No NIST endorsement,
official NIST distribution, or blanket licence to redistribute NIST databases
is claimed. This notice is not a licence for the project's original code.
